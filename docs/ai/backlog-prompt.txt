## Problem
Students borrowing microcontroller boards and test instruments have to visit the lab room in person and write in a paper logbook.
Neither students nor lab technicians can quickly check available stock or overdue items, causing project delays and lost equipment.

## Proposed outcome
Students browse available lab equipment by category, submit a loan request with a return date, and receive email reminders before the deadline.
Lab technicians review pending loan requests, approve or reject them, and record returns so stock counts stay accurate.

## Affected users and systems
Undergraduate engineering students (borrowers) and lab technicians (approvers/inventory managers).
Entities: Equipment, Borrower, and LoanRequest, plus external integration with the university SMTP email notification service.

## Constraints
Only institutional email addresses are accepted; no national ID numbers or sensitive personal data are stored.
Loan duration cannot exceed 14 days per request, and unavailable or broken items cannot be borrowed.

## Open questions
How many days before the due date should the reminder email be sent?
Can a student renew an active loan online if nobody else is waiting for that equipment?
